Anda Paratha

25 minBreakfast & Sehri

A short home-style guide to Anda Paratha. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Eggs (Anda)No eggs? Use paneer or firm tofu in curries and rice dishes.
  • Wheat flour (atta)No wheat flour (atta)? Use maida (plain flour), the result is softer.
  • Onion (Pyaz)No onion? Use spring onions, or a little onion powder (less depth).
  • Green chilli (Hari mirch)No green chilli? Use red chilli flakes or extra red chilli powder.

Method

  1. Make atta paratha dough and cook one side on a tawa.
  2. Flip, pour beaten egg mixed with onion, chilli and salt over the top and cook until the egg sets.
