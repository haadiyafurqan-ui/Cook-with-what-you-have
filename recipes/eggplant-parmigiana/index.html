Eggplant Parmigiana

60 minLunch & DinnerAir FryerVegetarian

A short home-style guide to Eggplant Parmigiana, with steps for the usual way and for the air fryer. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Eggplant (baingan)No eggplant (baingan)? Use tori or potato.
  • Tomato (Tamatar)No tomato? Use 1 tbsp tomato paste per tomato, or 2 tbsp yogurt for tang.
  • CheeseNo cheese? Use skip it, or use cheese spread.

In the oven

  1. Fry baingan slices, layer with tomato sauce and cheese, and bake until bubbling.

In the air fryer (about 50 min)

  1. Brush baingan slices with oil and air fry at 200°C for 10 to 12 minutes, turning halfway, instead of frying.
  2. Layer with tomato sauce and cheese in a small dish and air fry at 180°C for 8 to 10 minutes until bubbling.

Air fryer tip: Use a small tin or dish that fits the basket with a little room around it.

Air fryer basics: Heat the air fryer for 3 to 5 minutes first. Don't fill the basket more than halfway. Brush or spray a little oil. Turn or shake halfway. Use paper liners with holes. Air fryers vary, so check a few minutes early the first time.
