Beef Chilli Dry

45 minLunch & DinnerAir Fryer

A short home-style guide to Beef Chilli Dry, with steps for the usual way and for the air fryer. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Beef or mutton (gosht)No beef or mutton (gosht)? Use chicken (cook for less time).
  • CornflourNo cornflour? Use rice flour or plain flour (slightly less crisp).
  • Capsicum (Shimla mirch)No capsicum? Use extra cabbage or onion.
  • Onion (Pyaz)No onion? Use spring onions, or a little onion powder (less depth).
  • Soy sauceNo soy sauce? Use a little vinegar with pepper and extra salt (taste will differ).

The usual way: fried

  1. Fry cornflour-coated beef strips until crisp.
  2. Stir-fry onion, capsicum and chilli with soy and chilli sauce, then toss in the beef.

In the air fryer (about 45 min)

  1. Coat beef strips in cornflour, spray with oil and air fry at 200°C for 10 to 12 minutes, shaking halfway, until crisp.
  2. Stir-fry onion, capsicum and chilli with soy and chilli sauce, then toss in the beef.

Air fryer tip: Spray the coated pieces with oil before air frying, or the cornflour stays white and powdery.

Air fryer basics: Heat the air fryer for 3 to 5 minutes first. Don't fill the basket more than halfway. Brush or spray a little oil. Turn or shake halfway. Use paper liners with holes. Air fryers vary, so check a few minutes early the first time.
