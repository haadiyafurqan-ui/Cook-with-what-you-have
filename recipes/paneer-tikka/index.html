Paneer Tikka

35 minAir FryerSpecial OccasionsVegetarian

A short home-style guide to Paneer Tikka, with steps for the usual way and for the air fryer. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • PaneerNo paneer? Use firm tofu.
  • Capsicum (Shimla mirch)No capsicum? Use extra cabbage or onion.
  • Onion (Pyaz)No onion? Use spring onions, or a little onion powder (less depth).
  • Yogurt (Dahi)No yogurt? Use milk with 1 tsp lemon juice, rested 10 minutes.

On the grill

  1. Marinate paneer cubes with capsicum and onion in yogurt and tikka masala.
  2. Skewer and grill or pan-cook until charred.

In the air fryer (about 30 min)

  1. Marinate paneer cubes with capsicum and onion in yogurt and tikka masala and skewer.
  2. Air fry at 200°C for 8 to 10 minutes, turning halfway, until charred at the edges.

Air fryer tip: Use short skewers that fit inside the basket, or cook the pieces without skewers.

Air fryer basics: Heat the air fryer for 3 to 5 minutes first. Don't fill the basket more than halfway. Brush or spray a little oil. Turn or shake halfway. Use paper liners with holes. Air fryers vary, so check a few minutes early the first time.
