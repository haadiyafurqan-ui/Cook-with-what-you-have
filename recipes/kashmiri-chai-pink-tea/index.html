Kashmiri Chai (Pink Tea)

30 minDessertsVegetarian

A short home-style guide to Kashmiri Chai (Pink Tea). Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Milk (Doodh)No milk? Use milk powder mixed with water, or diluted tinned milk.
  • PistachiosNo pistachios? Use almonds or cashews.

Method

  1. Boil Kashmiri tea leaves with a pinch of baking soda until deep red, add milk and salt and whisk until pink.
  2. Top with crushed pistachios.
