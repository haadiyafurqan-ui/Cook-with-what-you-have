Mango Kulfi (Peshawari Ice Cream)

60 minDessertsVegetarian

A short home-style guide to Mango Kulfi (Peshawari Ice Cream). Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Milk (Doodh)No milk? Use milk powder mixed with water, or diluted tinned milk.
  • Mango (Aam)No mango? Use canned mango pulp.
  • Cream (Malai (cream))No cream? Use milk with a little butter, simmered to thicken (lighter).
  • Sugar (Cheeni)No sugar? Use jaggery (gur) or honey, added gradually.

Method

  1. Reduce milk with sugar and cool, then blend with mango pulp and cream.
  2. Pour into moulds with sticks and freeze until firm.
