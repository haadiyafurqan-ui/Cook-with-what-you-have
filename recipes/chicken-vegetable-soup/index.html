Chicken Vegetable Soup

35 minLunch & DinnerSoups & Sides

A short home-style guide to Chicken Vegetable Soup. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Chicken (Murghi)No chicken? Use mutton or beef (cook longer), or soya chunks.
  • Carrot (Gajar)No carrot? Use extra cabbage or capsicum.
  • Onion (Pyaz)No onion? Use spring onions, or a little onion powder (less depth).

Method

  1. Boil chicken with onion, salt and pepper until tender, then shred it.
  2. Add diced carrot, simmer until soft and serve hot with black pepper.
