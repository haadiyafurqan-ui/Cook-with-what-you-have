Manchow Soup

30 minLunch & DinnerSoups & SidesVegetarian

A short home-style guide to Manchow Soup. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Cabbage (Band gobi)No cabbage? Use extra carrot or capsicum.
  • Carrot (Gajar)No carrot? Use extra cabbage or capsicum.
  • Soy sauceNo soy sauce? Use a little vinegar with pepper and extra salt (taste will differ).
  • CornflourNo cornflour? Use rice flour or plain flour (slightly less crisp).

Method

  1. Fry vegetables with garlic, add vegetable stock, soy sauce and pepper, thicken with cornflour and serve with crispy noodles.
