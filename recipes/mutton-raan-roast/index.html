Mutton Raan Roast

180 minNeeds an ovenSpecial Occasions

A short home-style guide to Mutton Raan Roast. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Beef or mutton (gosht)No beef or mutton (gosht)? Use chicken (cook for less time).
  • Yogurt (Dahi)No yogurt? Use milk with 1 tsp lemon juice, rested 10 minutes.

Method

  1. Marinate a leg of mutton with yogurt, raw papaya and spices overnight, then slow-roast until tender.

Not for the air fryer. It is too big for most air fryer baskets. Use the oven.
