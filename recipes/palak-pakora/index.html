Palak Pakora

25 minIftar & SnacksVegetarian

A short home-style guide to Palak Pakora. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Gram flour (besan)No gram flour (besan)? Use finely ground chana daal or rice flour (less flavour).
  • Spinach (Palak)No spinach? Use frozen spinach, methi or any leafy greens.
  • Green chilli (Hari mirch)No green chilli? Use red chilli flakes or extra red chilli powder.

Method

  1. Dip palak leaves or chopped palak into a thick spiced besan batter and deep-fry until crisp.

Not for the air fryer. The batter is runny, so it drips through the basket. Fry this one.
