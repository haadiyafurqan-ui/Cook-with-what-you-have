Chicken Noodle Soup

35 minLunch & DinnerSoups & Sides

A short home-style guide to Chicken Noodle Soup. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Chicken (Murghi)No chicken? Use mutton or beef (cook longer), or soya chunks.
  • NoodlesNo noodles? Use thin spaghetti, boiled.
  • Carrot (Gajar)No carrot? Use extra cabbage or capsicum.
  • Onion (Pyaz)No onion? Use spring onions, or a little onion powder (less depth).

Method

  1. Boil chicken with onion, salt and pepper and shred it.
  2. Add carrot and noodles to the stock and cook until soft.
