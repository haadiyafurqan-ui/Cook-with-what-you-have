Fruit and Yogurt Parfait

10 minBreakfast & SehriQuick (20 min or less)Vegetarian

A short home-style guide to Fruit and Yogurt Parfait. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Yogurt (Dahi)No yogurt? Use milk with 1 tsp lemon juice, rested 10 minutes.
  • Apple (Seb)No apple? Use pear or any seasonal fruit.
  • Banana (Kela)No banana? Use skip it, or use apple or dates.
  • Honey (Shehad)No honey? Use sugar melted with a little water.

Method

  1. Layer yogurt with chopped fruit and a little honey in a glass.
  2. Top with crushed nuts if you like.
