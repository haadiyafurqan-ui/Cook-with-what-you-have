Tres Leches Cake

75 minDesserts

A short home-style guide to Tres Leches Cake. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Plain flour (maida)No plain flour (maida)? Use atta, sifted (the result is denser).
  • Eggs (Anda)No eggs? Use paneer or firm tofu in curries and rice dishes.
  • Milk (Doodh)No milk? Use milk powder mixed with water, or diluted tinned milk.
  • Condensed milkNo condensed milk? Use milk powder with milk and sugar, simmered thick.
  • Cream (Malai (cream))No cream? Use milk with a little butter, simmered to thicken (lighter).
  • Sugar (Cheeni)No sugar? Use jaggery (gur) or honey, added gradually.
  • Baking powderNo baking powder? Use a pinch of baking soda with a squeeze of lemon, or skip it (the result will be denser).

Method

  1. Bake a light sponge with maida, eggs, sugar and baking powder.
  2. Soak with milk and condensed milk, chill, then top with whipped cream.

Not for the air fryer. It is too big for most air fryer baskets. Use the oven.
