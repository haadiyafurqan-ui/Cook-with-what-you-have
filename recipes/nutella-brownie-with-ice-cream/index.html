Nutella Brownie with Ice Cream

45 minAir FryerDesserts

A short home-style guide to Nutella Brownie with Ice Cream, with steps for the usual way and for the air fryer. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Plain flour (maida)No plain flour (maida)? Use atta, sifted (the result is denser).
  • Eggs (Anda)No eggs? Use paneer or firm tofu in curries and rice dishes.
  • Butter (Makhan)No butter? Use ghee or oil.
  • Sugar (Cheeni)No sugar? Use jaggery (gur) or honey, added gradually.
  • Cocoa powderNo cocoa powder? Use melted dark chocolate (use less butter).
  • Hazelnut chocolate spread (Nutella)No hazelnut chocolate spread? Use melted chocolate with chopped nuts.
  • Ice creamNo ice cream? Use whipped cream or kulfi.

In the oven

  1. Make brownie batter with butter, sugar, eggs, maida and cocoa, swirl in nutella and bake.
  2. Serve warm with vanilla ice cream.

In the air fryer (about 45 min)

  1. Make brownie batter with butter, sugar, eggs, maida and cocoa, swirl in nutella and pour into a small lined tin.
  2. Air fry at 160°C for 22 to 25 minutes until just set.
  3. Serve warm with vanilla ice cream.

Air fryer tip: Use a small tin or dish that fits the basket with a little room around it.

Air fryer basics: Heat the air fryer for 3 to 5 minutes first. Don't fill the basket more than halfway. Brush or spray a little oil. Turn or shake halfway. Use paper liners with holes. Air fryers vary, so check a few minutes early the first time.
