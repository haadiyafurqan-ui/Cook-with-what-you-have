Chicken Pakora

30 minIftar & Snacks

A short home-style guide to Chicken Pakora. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Chicken (Murghi)No chicken? Use mutton or beef (cook longer), or soya chunks.
  • Gram flour (besan)No gram flour (besan)? Use finely ground chana daal or rice flour (less flavour).
  • Green chilli (Hari mirch)No green chilli? Use red chilli flakes or extra red chilli powder.

Method

  1. Marinate chicken pieces in ginger-garlic, spices and lemon, coat in besan batter and deep-fry until cooked through.

Not for the air fryer. The batter is runny, so it drips through the basket. Fry this one.
