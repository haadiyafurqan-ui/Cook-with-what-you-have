Vegetable Spring Rolls

40 minLunch & DinnerIftar & SnacksAir FryerVegetarian

A short home-style guide to Vegetable Spring Rolls, with steps for the usual way and for the air fryer. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Cabbage (Band gobi)No cabbage? Use extra carrot or capsicum.
  • Carrot (Gajar)No carrot? Use extra cabbage or capsicum.
  • Capsicum (Shimla mirch)No capsicum? Use extra cabbage or onion.
  • Plain flour (maida)No plain flour (maida)? Use atta, sifted (the result is denser).
  • Soy sauceNo soy sauce? Use a little vinegar with pepper and extra salt (taste will differ).

The usual way: fried

  1. Stir-fry shredded cabbage, carrot and capsicum with soy sauce and pepper and cool.
  2. Roll in spring roll sheets, seal with maida paste and deep-fry until golden.

In the air fryer (about 35 min)

  1. Stir-fry shredded cabbage, carrot and capsicum with soy sauce and pepper and cool.
  2. Roll in spring roll sheets and seal with maida paste.
  3. Brush with oil and air fry at 190°C for 10 to 12 minutes, turning halfway, until golden.

Air fryer tip: Let the filling cool completely, or the sheets go soggy.

Air fryer basics: Heat the air fryer for 3 to 5 minutes first. Don't fill the basket more than halfway. Brush or spray a little oil. Turn or shake halfway. Use paper liners with holes. Air fryers vary, so check a few minutes early the first time.
