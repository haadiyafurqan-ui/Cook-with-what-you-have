Vegetable Manchurian

35 minLunch & DinnerAir FryerVegetarian

A short home-style guide to Vegetable Manchurian, with steps for the usual way and for the air fryer. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Cabbage (Band gobi)No cabbage? Use extra carrot or capsicum.
  • Carrot (Gajar)No carrot? Use extra cabbage or capsicum.
  • CornflourNo cornflour? Use rice flour or plain flour (slightly less crisp).
  • Soy sauceNo soy sauce? Use a little vinegar with pepper and extra salt (taste will differ).
  • Onion (Pyaz)No onion? Use spring onions, or a little onion powder (less depth).

The usual way: fried

  1. Mix grated cabbage and carrot with cornflour, salt and pepper, shape into balls and fry until golden.
  2. Simmer in a garlic, soy and chilli sauce gravy thickened with cornflour.

In the air fryer (about 35 min)

  1. Squeeze the water out of grated cabbage and carrot, then mix with cornflour, salt and pepper and shape into balls.
  2. Spray with oil and air fry at 190°C for 12 to 15 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden.
  3. Simmer in a garlic, soy and chilli sauce gravy thickened with cornflour.

Air fryer tip: Squeeze the vegetables well, or the balls stay soft and fall apart.

Air fryer basics: Heat the air fryer for 3 to 5 minutes first. Don't fill the basket more than halfway. Brush or spray a little oil. Turn or shake halfway. Use paper liners with holes. Air fryers vary, so check a few minutes early the first time.
