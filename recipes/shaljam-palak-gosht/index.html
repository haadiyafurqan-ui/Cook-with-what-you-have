Shaljam Palak Gosht

65 minLunch & Dinner

A short home-style guide to Shaljam Palak Gosht. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Beef or mutton (gosht)No beef or mutton (gosht)? Use chicken (cook for less time).
  • Turnip (Shaljam)No turnip? Use radish or potato.
  • Spinach (Palak)No spinach? Use frozen spinach, methi or any leafy greens.
  • Onion (Pyaz)No onion? Use spring onions, or a little onion powder (less depth).

Method

  1. Cook gosht with onion and masalas until nearly tender.
  2. Add turnip pieces, then chopped palak, and cook until soft and the oil separates.
