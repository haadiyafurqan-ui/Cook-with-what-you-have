Carrot Soup

30 minSoups & SidesVegetarian

A short home-style guide to Carrot Soup. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Carrot (Gajar)No carrot? Use extra cabbage or capsicum.
  • Onion (Pyaz)No onion? Use spring onions, or a little onion powder (less depth).

Method

  1. Cook carrot and onion in water until soft, blend smooth, and season with salt, pepper and a little butter.
