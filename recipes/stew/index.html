Stew

80 minLunch & DinnerSpecial Occasions

A short home-style guide to Stew. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Beef or mutton (gosht)No beef or mutton (gosht)? Use chicken (cook for less time).
  • Potato (Aloo)No potato? Use sweet potato or turnip (shaljam).
  • Carrot (Gajar)No carrot? Use extra cabbage or capsicum.
  • Onion (Pyaz)No onion? Use spring onions, or a little onion powder (less depth).

Method

  1. Brown gosht with onion, ginger-garlic, salt and whole spices; add water and simmer until nearly tender.
  2. Add potato and carrot chunks, cook until soft and finish with black pepper.
