Veggie Sandwich

10 minIftar & SnacksQuick (20 min or less)Vegetarian

A short home-style guide to Veggie Sandwich. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Bread (Double roti)No bread? Use roti or paratha as a wrap.
  • Tomato (Tamatar)No tomato? Use 1 tbsp tomato paste per tomato, or 2 tbsp yogurt for tang.
  • Cucumber (Kheera)No cucumber? Use extra tomato or raw carrot.
  • Capsicum (Shimla mirch)No capsicum? Use extra cabbage or onion.

Method

  1. Butter the bread, layer sliced tomato, cucumber and capsicum with salt and pepper, and cut into triangles.
