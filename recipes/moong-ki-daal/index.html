Moong ki Daal

25 minBreakfast & SehriLunch & DinnerVegetarian

A short home-style guide to Moong ki Daal. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Moong lentils (Moong daal)No moong lentils? Use another daal (cooking time changes).
  • Onion (Pyaz)No onion? Use spring onions, or a little onion powder (less depth).
  • Tomato (Tamatar)No tomato? Use 1 tbsp tomato paste per tomato, or 2 tbsp yogurt for tang.

Method

  1. Boil washed moong daal with turmeric until soft.
  2. Fry onion and tomato with cumin and chilli, then stir into the daal.
