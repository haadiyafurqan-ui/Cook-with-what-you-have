Barbecue Pulled Beef Sandwich

180 minIftar & Snacks

A short home-style guide to Barbecue Pulled Beef Sandwich. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Burger bunsNo burger buns? Use bread slices or roti.
  • Beef or mutton (gosht)No beef or mutton (gosht)? Use chicken (cook for less time).
  • Onion (Pyaz)No onion? Use spring onions, or a little onion powder (less depth).

Method

  1. Slow-cook beef with onion and spices until it shreds.
  2. Toss in BBQ sauce and pile into buns.
