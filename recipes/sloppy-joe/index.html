Sloppy Joe

30 minLunch & DinnerIftar & Snacks

A short home-style guide to Sloppy Joe. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Burger bunsNo burger buns? Use bread slices or roti.
  • Mince (qeema)No mince (qeema)? Use finely chopped chicken or soya granules.
  • Onion (Pyaz)No onion? Use spring onions, or a little onion powder (less depth).
  • Tomato (Tamatar)No tomato? Use 1 tbsp tomato paste per tomato, or 2 tbsp yogurt for tang.

Method

  1. Cook qeema with onion and tomato into a saucy filling and pile into buns.
