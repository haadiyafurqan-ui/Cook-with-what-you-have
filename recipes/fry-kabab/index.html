Fry Kabab

30 minIftar & SnacksAir FryerSpecial Occasions

A short home-style guide to Fry Kabab, with steps for the usual way and for the air fryer. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Mince (qeema)No mince (qeema)? Use finely chopped chicken or soya granules.
  • Eggs (Anda)No eggs? Use paneer or firm tofu in curries and rice dishes.
  • Onion (Pyaz)No onion? Use spring onions, or a little onion powder (less depth).
  • Green chilli (Hari mirch)No green chilli? Use red chilli flakes or extra red chilli powder.

The usual way: fried

  1. Mix qeema with egg, chopped onion, green chilli, spices and salt.
  2. Shape into flat patties and shallow-fry until browned on both sides.

In the air fryer (about 30 min)

  1. Mix qeema with egg, chopped onion, green chilli, spices and salt.
  2. Shape into flat patties, brush with oil and air fry at 200°C for 12 to 14 minutes, turning halfway, until browned and cooked through.

Air fryer basics: Heat the air fryer for 3 to 5 minutes first. Don't fill the basket more than halfway. Brush or spray a little oil. Turn or shake halfway. Use paper liners with holes. Air fryers vary, so check a few minutes early the first time.
