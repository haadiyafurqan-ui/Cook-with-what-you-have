Chicken Cutlets

45 minIftar & SnacksAir Fryer

A short home-style guide to Chicken Cutlets, with steps for the usual way and for the air fryer. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Chicken (Murghi)No chicken? Use mutton or beef (cook longer), or soya chunks.
  • Potato (Aloo)No potato? Use sweet potato or turnip (shaljam).
  • Bread (Double roti)No bread? Use roti or paratha as a wrap.
  • Eggs (Anda)No eggs? Use paneer or firm tofu in curries and rice dishes.

The usual way: fried

  1. Mix boiled shredded chicken with mashed potato, spices and chopped chilli.
  2. Shape, dip in egg, coat in breadcrumbs and fry.

In the air fryer (about 40 min)

  1. Mix boiled shredded chicken with mashed potato, spices and chopped chilli.
  2. Shape, dip in egg and coat in breadcrumbs.
  3. Spray with oil and air fry at 200°C for 10 to 12 minutes, turning halfway, until golden.

Air fryer basics: Heat the air fryer for 3 to 5 minutes first. Don't fill the basket more than halfway. Brush or spray a little oil. Turn or shake halfway. Use paper liners with holes. Air fryers vary, so check a few minutes early the first time.
