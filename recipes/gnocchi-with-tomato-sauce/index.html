Gnocchi with Tomato Sauce

60 minLunch & DinnerVegetarian

A short home-style guide to Gnocchi with Tomato Sauce. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Potato (Aloo)No potato? Use sweet potato or turnip (shaljam).
  • Plain flour (maida)No plain flour (maida)? Use atta, sifted (the result is denser).
  • Tomato (Tamatar)No tomato? Use 1 tbsp tomato paste per tomato, or 2 tbsp yogurt for tang.

Method

  1. Mix mashed potato with maida into a dough, shape and boil until they float.
  2. Toss in tomato sauce.
