Aloo Mungray

35 minLunch & DinnerVegetarian

A short home-style guide to Aloo Mungray. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Potato (Aloo)No potato? Use sweet potato or turnip (shaljam).
  • Radish pods (mungray)No radish pods (mungray)? Use skip, or use radish.
  • Onion (Pyaz)No onion? Use spring onions, or a little onion powder (less depth).
  • Tomato (Tamatar)No tomato? Use 1 tbsp tomato paste per tomato, or 2 tbsp yogurt for tang.

Method

  1. Fry onion and tomato with masalas into a thick base.
  2. Add potato and mungray and a splash of water, cover and cook on low until soft, then dry off any extra water.
