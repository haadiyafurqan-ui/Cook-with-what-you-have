Dum ka Qeema

50 minSpecial Occasions

A short home-style guide to Dum ka Qeema. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Mince (qeema)No mince (qeema)? Use finely chopped chicken or soya granules.
  • Onion (Pyaz)No onion? Use spring onions, or a little onion powder (less depth).
  • Yogurt (Dahi)No yogurt? Use milk with 1 tsp lemon juice, rested 10 minutes.

Method

  1. Fry onion until golden, add qeema, ginger-garlic, yogurt and masalas and cook until dry.
  2. Seal the pot and cook on the lowest heat (dum) for 15 minutes.
