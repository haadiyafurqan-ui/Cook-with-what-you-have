Mutton Matka Gosht

120 minSpecial Occasions

A short home-style guide to Mutton Matka Gosht. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Beef or mutton (gosht)No beef or mutton (gosht)? Use chicken (cook for less time).
  • Yogurt (Dahi)No yogurt? Use milk with 1 tsp lemon juice, rested 10 minutes.
  • Onion (Pyaz)No onion? Use spring onions, or a little onion powder (less depth).
  • Tomato (Tamatar)No tomato? Use 1 tbsp tomato paste per tomato, or 2 tbsp yogurt for tang.

Method

  1. Layer gosht with onion, tomato, yogurt and spices in a clay pot, seal and slow-cook until tender.
