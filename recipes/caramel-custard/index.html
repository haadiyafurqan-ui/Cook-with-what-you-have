Caramel Custard

60 minAir FryerDesserts

A short home-style guide to Caramel Custard, with steps for the usual way and for the air fryer. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Eggs (Anda)No eggs? Use paneer or firm tofu in curries and rice dishes.
  • Milk (Doodh)No milk? Use milk powder mixed with water, or diluted tinned milk.
  • Sugar (Cheeni)No sugar? Use jaggery (gur) or honey, added gradually.

In the oven

  1. Melt sugar into caramel in a mould.
  2. Pour in whisked eggs, milk and sugar and steam or bake gently until set, then chill and unmould.

In the air fryer (about 50 min)

  1. Melt sugar into caramel in small ramekins.
  2. Pour in whisked eggs, milk and sugar, cover each with foil and air fry at 150°C for 18 to 22 minutes until set with a slight wobble.
  3. Chill and unmould.

Air fryer tip: Small ramekins work best. A big mould won't set evenly in the air fryer.

Air fryer basics: Heat the air fryer for 3 to 5 minutes first. Don't fill the basket more than halfway. Brush or spray a little oil. Turn or shake halfway. Use paper liners with holes. Air fryers vary, so check a few minutes early the first time.
