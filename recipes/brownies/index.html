Brownies

40 minAir FryerDesserts

A short home-style guide to Brownies, with steps for the usual way and for the air fryer. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Plain flour (maida)No plain flour (maida)? Use atta, sifted (the result is denser).
  • Eggs (Anda)No eggs? Use paneer or firm tofu in curries and rice dishes.
  • Butter (Makhan)No butter? Use ghee or oil.
  • Sugar (Cheeni)No sugar? Use jaggery (gur) or honey, added gradually.
  • Cocoa powderNo cocoa powder? Use melted dark chocolate (use less butter).

In the oven

  1. Whisk melted butter, sugar and eggs, fold in maida and cocoa, pour into a tray and bake until the centre is just set.

In the air fryer (about 40 min)

  1. Whisk melted butter, sugar and eggs, fold in maida and cocoa and pour into a small lined tin.
  2. Air fry at 160°C for 22 to 25 minutes until the centre is just set.

Air fryer tip: Use a small tin or dish that fits the basket with a little room around it.

Air fryer basics: Heat the air fryer for 3 to 5 minutes first. Don't fill the basket more than halfway. Brush or spray a little oil. Turn or shake halfway. Use paper liners with holes. Air fryers vary, so check a few minutes early the first time.
