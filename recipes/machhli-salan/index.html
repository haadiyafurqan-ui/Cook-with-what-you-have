Machhli Salan

40 minLunch & Dinner

A short home-style guide to Machhli Salan. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Fish (Machhli)No fish? Use prawns or chicken.
  • Onion (Pyaz)No onion? Use spring onions, or a little onion powder (less depth).
  • Tomato (Tamatar)No tomato? Use 1 tbsp tomato paste per tomato, or 2 tbsp yogurt for tang.

Method

  1. Marinate fish with salt, turmeric and lemon, then fry lightly.
  2. Cook an onion-tomato masala with a little water and simmer the fish gently for 8 minutes.
