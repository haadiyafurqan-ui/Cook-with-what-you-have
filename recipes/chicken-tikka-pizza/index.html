Chicken Tikka Pizza

90 minLunch & DinnerAir Fryer

A short home-style guide to Chicken Tikka Pizza, with steps for the usual way and for the air fryer. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Plain flour (maida)No plain flour (maida)? Use atta, sifted (the result is denser).
  • Yeast (Khameer (yeast))No yeast? Use baking powder dough (not as fluffy).
  • Chicken (Murghi)No chicken? Use mutton or beef (cook longer), or soya chunks.
  • CheeseNo cheese? Use skip it, or use cheese spread.
  • Onion (Pyaz)No onion? Use spring onions, or a little onion powder (less depth).
  • Tomato (Tamatar)No tomato? Use 1 tbsp tomato paste per tomato, or 2 tbsp yogurt for tang.

In the oven

  1. Top pizza dough with tomato sauce, tikka chicken, onion and cheese and bake until golden.

In the air fryer (about 90 min)

  1. Roll pizza dough into small rounds that fit the basket.
  2. Air fry the base at 200°C for 3 minutes, top with tomato sauce, tikka chicken, onion and cheese, and air fry 6 to 8 minutes more until golden.

Air fryer tip: Make small pizzas that fit the basket and cook one at a time on a paper liner.

Air fryer basics: Heat the air fryer for 3 to 5 minutes first. Don't fill the basket more than halfway. Brush or spray a little oil. Turn or shake halfway. Use paper liners with holes. Air fryers vary, so check a few minutes early the first time.
