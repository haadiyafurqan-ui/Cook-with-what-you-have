Margherita Pizza

90 minLunch & DinnerAir FryerVegetarian

A short home-style guide to Margherita Pizza, with steps for the usual way and for the air fryer. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Plain flour (maida)No plain flour (maida)? Use atta, sifted (the result is denser).
  • Yeast (Khameer (yeast))No yeast? Use baking powder dough (not as fluffy).
  • Tomato (Tamatar)No tomato? Use 1 tbsp tomato paste per tomato, or 2 tbsp yogurt for tang.
  • CheeseNo cheese? Use skip it, or use cheese spread.
  • BasilNo basil? Use fresh coriander, or dried oregano.

In the oven

  1. Knead maida with yeast, salt and warm water, rest 1 hour, then roll out.
  2. Top with tomato sauce, cheese and basil and bake until crisp.

In the air fryer (about 90 min)

  1. Knead maida with yeast, salt and warm water, rest 1 hour and roll into small rounds that fit the basket.
  2. Air fry the base at 200°C for 3 minutes, add tomato sauce, cheese and basil, and air fry 5 to 7 minutes more until crisp.

Air fryer tip: Make small pizzas that fit the basket and cook one at a time on a paper liner.

Air fryer basics: Heat the air fryer for 3 to 5 minutes first. Don't fill the basket more than halfway. Brush or spray a little oil. Turn or shake halfway. Use paper liners with holes. Air fryers vary, so check a few minutes early the first time.
