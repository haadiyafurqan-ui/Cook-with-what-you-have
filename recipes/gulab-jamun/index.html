Gulab Jamun

50 minDessertsSpecial OccasionsVegetarian

A short home-style guide to Gulab Jamun. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Milk powder (Doodh ka powder)No milk powder? Use khoya (mawa).
  • Plain flour (maida)No plain flour (maida)? Use atta, sifted (the result is denser).
  • Sugar (Cheeni)No sugar? Use jaggery (gur) or honey, added gradually.

Method

  1. Knead milk powder, maida and a little milk into a soft dough, shape into balls and fry slowly until golden.
  2. Soak in warm sugar syrup.

Not for the air fryer. It needs to fry slowly in oil to cook through and stay soft. In an air fryer it dries out.
