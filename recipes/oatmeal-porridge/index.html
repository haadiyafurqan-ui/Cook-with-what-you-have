Oatmeal Porridge

10 minBreakfast & SehriQuick (20 min or less)Vegetarian

A short home-style guide to Oatmeal Porridge. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • OatsNo oats? Use daliya (cook longer).
  • Milk (Doodh)No milk? Use milk powder mixed with water, or diluted tinned milk.
  • Banana (Kela)No banana? Use skip it, or use apple or dates.

Method

  1. Simmer oats in milk, stirring, until thick and creamy.
  2. Top with sliced banana and sugar or honey if you like.
