Spaghetti Aglio e Olio

20 minLunch & DinnerQuick (20 min or less)Vegetarian

A short home-style guide to Spaghetti Aglio e Olio. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • PastaNo pasta? Use macaroni or boiled spaghetti.

Method

  1. Boil spaghetti.
  2. Gently fry plenty of sliced garlic and chilli flakes in olive oil, then toss in the pasta with a splash of its water and salt.
