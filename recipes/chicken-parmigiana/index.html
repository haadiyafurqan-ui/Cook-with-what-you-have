Chicken Parmigiana

45 minLunch & DinnerAir Fryer

A short home-style guide to Chicken Parmigiana, with steps for the usual way and for the air fryer. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Chicken (Murghi)No chicken? Use mutton or beef (cook longer), or soya chunks.
  • Eggs (Anda)No eggs? Use paneer or firm tofu in curries and rice dishes.
  • Bread (Double roti)No bread? Use roti or paratha as a wrap.
  • CheeseNo cheese? Use skip it, or use cheese spread.
  • Tomato (Tamatar)No tomato? Use 1 tbsp tomato paste per tomato, or 2 tbsp yogurt for tang.

The usual way: fried

  1. Coat flattened chicken in egg and breadcrumbs and fry.
  2. Top with tomato sauce and cheese and bake until melted.

In the air fryer (about 35 min)

  1. Coat flattened chicken in egg and breadcrumbs, spray with oil and air fry at 200°C for 10 to 12 minutes, turning halfway.
  2. Top with tomato sauce and cheese and air fry 3 to 4 minutes more until melted.

Air fryer basics: Heat the air fryer for 3 to 5 minutes first. Don't fill the basket more than halfway. Brush or spray a little oil. Turn or shake halfway. Use paper liners with holes. Air fryers vary, so check a few minutes early the first time.
