Grilled Cheese Sandwich

10 minLunch & DinnerAir FryerQuick (20 min or less)Vegetarian

A short home-style guide to Grilled Cheese Sandwich, with steps for the usual way and for the air fryer. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Bread (Double roti)No bread? Use roti or paratha as a wrap.
  • CheeseNo cheese? Use skip it, or use cheese spread.
  • Butter (Makhan)No butter? Use ghee or oil.
  • Tomato (Tamatar)No tomato? Use 1 tbsp tomato paste per tomato, or 2 tbsp yogurt for tang.

In a pan

  1. Butter the bread and put cheese and thin tomato slices between two slices.
  2. Toast on a pan until golden and the cheese melts.

In the air fryer (about 10 min)

  1. Butter the outside of the bread and put cheese and thin tomato slices between two slices.
  2. Air fry at 180°C for 6 to 8 minutes, turning halfway, until golden and the cheese melts.

Air fryer tip: Pin the top slice with a toothpick, or the fan can lift it off.

Air fryer basics: Heat the air fryer for 3 to 5 minutes first. Don't fill the basket more than halfway. Brush or spray a little oil. Turn or shake halfway. Use paper liners with holes. Air fryers vary, so check a few minutes early the first time.
