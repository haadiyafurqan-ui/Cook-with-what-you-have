Spanish Omelette

30 minBreakfast & SehriLunch & Dinner

A short home-style guide to Spanish Omelette. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Eggs (Anda)No eggs? Use paneer or firm tofu in curries and rice dishes.
  • Potato (Aloo)No potato? Use sweet potato or turnip (shaljam).
  • Onion (Pyaz)No onion? Use spring onions, or a little onion powder (less depth).

Method

  1. Fry thin potato slices and onion in oil until soft, then mix them into beaten eggs with salt.
  2. Cook in a pan on low heat until set, flip once and serve in wedges.
