Kalay Masoor Chawal

45 minLunch & DinnerVegetarian

A short home-style guide to Kalay Masoor Chawal. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Whole black masoor (Kalay masoor)No whole black masoor? Use regular masoor daal (cooks faster).
  • Rice (Chawal)No rice? Use any other rice, or broken rice (cooks faster).
  • Onion (Pyaz)No onion? Use spring onions, or a little onion powder (less depth).

Method

  1. Boil whole kalay masoor with turmeric and salt until soft.
  2. Finish with a tarka of fried onion, garlic and cumin; serve with rice if you like.
