Matar Qeema

40 minLunch & DinnerSpecial Occasions

A short home-style guide to Matar Qeema. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Mince (qeema)No mince (qeema)? Use finely chopped chicken or soya granules.
  • Peas (Matar)No peas? Use diced carrot or green beans.
  • Onion (Pyaz)No onion? Use spring onions, or a little onion powder (less depth).
  • Tomato (Tamatar)No tomato? Use 1 tbsp tomato paste per tomato, or 2 tbsp yogurt for tang.

Method

  1. Fry onion with ginger-garlic, add qeema and masalas and cook until browned.
  2. Add peas and tomato and a little water; cover and cook until soft and dry.
