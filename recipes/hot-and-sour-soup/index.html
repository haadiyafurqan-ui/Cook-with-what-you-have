Hot and Sour Soup

30 minLunch & DinnerSoups & Sides

A short home-style guide to Hot and Sour Soup. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Chicken (Murghi)No chicken? Use mutton or beef (cook longer), or soya chunks.
  • Eggs (Anda)No eggs? Use paneer or firm tofu in curries and rice dishes.
  • CornflourNo cornflour? Use rice flour or plain flour (slightly less crisp).
  • Soy sauceNo soy sauce? Use a little vinegar with pepper and extra salt (taste will differ).
  • Carrot (Gajar)No carrot? Use extra cabbage or capsicum.

Method

  1. Simmer chicken stock with shredded chicken, carrot, soy sauce, vinegar and pepper.
  2. Thicken with cornflour-water and stir in beaten egg in a thin stream.
