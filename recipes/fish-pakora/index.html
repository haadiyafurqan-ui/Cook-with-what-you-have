Fish Pakora

30 minIftar & Snacks

A short home-style guide to Fish Pakora. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Fish (Machhli)No fish? Use prawns or chicken.
  • Gram flour (besan)No gram flour (besan)? Use finely ground chana daal or rice flour (less flavour).

Method

  1. Marinate fish pieces in ajwain, chilli and lemon, coat in besan batter and deep-fry until crisp.

Not for the air fryer. The batter is runny, so it drips through the basket. Fry this one.
