Egg in a Basket

10 minBreakfast & SehriQuick (20 min or less)

A short home-style guide to Egg in a Basket. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Bread (Double roti)No bread? Use roti or paratha as a wrap.
  • Eggs (Anda)No eggs? Use paneer or firm tofu in curries and rice dishes.
  • Butter (Makhan)No butter? Use ghee or oil.

Method

  1. Cut a hole in a bread slice, fry it in butter, then crack an egg into the hole and cook until set.
