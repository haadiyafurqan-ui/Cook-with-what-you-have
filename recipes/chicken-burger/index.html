Chicken Burger

35 minIftar & SnacksAir Fryer

A short home-style guide to Chicken Burger, with steps for the usual way and for the air fryer. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Burger bunsNo burger buns? Use bread slices or roti.
  • Chicken (Murghi)No chicken? Use mutton or beef (cook longer), or soya chunks.
  • Eggs (Anda)No eggs? Use paneer or firm tofu in curries and rice dishes.
  • MayonnaiseNo mayonnaise? Use thick hung yogurt with salt and pepper.

The usual way: fried

  1. Coat chicken in egg and breadcrumbs and fry until crisp.
  2. Serve in a bun with mayonnaise and lettuce.

In the air fryer (about 35 min)

  1. Coat chicken in egg and breadcrumbs.
  2. Spray with oil and air fry at 190°C for 14 to 16 minutes, turning halfway, until crisp and cooked through.
  3. Serve in a bun with mayonnaise and lettuce.

Air fryer basics: Heat the air fryer for 3 to 5 minutes first. Don't fill the basket more than halfway. Brush or spray a little oil. Turn or shake halfway. Use paper liners with holes. Air fryers vary, so check a few minutes early the first time.
