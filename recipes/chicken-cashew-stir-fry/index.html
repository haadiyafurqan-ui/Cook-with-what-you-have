Chicken Cashew Stir-Fry

30 minLunch & Dinner

A short home-style guide to Chicken Cashew Stir-Fry. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Chicken (Murghi)No chicken? Use mutton or beef (cook longer), or soya chunks.
  • Dry fruit and nuts (Meva)No dry fruit and nuts? Use almonds, raisins or any nuts you have, or skip.
  • Capsicum (Shimla mirch)No capsicum? Use extra cabbage or onion.
  • Soy sauceNo soy sauce? Use a little vinegar with pepper and extra salt (taste will differ).

Method

  1. Stir-fry chicken with capsicum, soy sauce and garlic, then toss in roasted cashews at the end.
