Lasagna

75 minNeeds an ovenLunch & Dinner

A short home-style guide to Lasagna. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • PastaNo pasta? Use macaroni or boiled spaghetti.
  • Mince (qeema)No mince (qeema)? Use finely chopped chicken or soya granules.
  • CheeseNo cheese? Use skip it, or use cheese spread.
  • Tomato (Tamatar)No tomato? Use 1 tbsp tomato paste per tomato, or 2 tbsp yogurt for tang.
  • Milk (Doodh)No milk? Use milk powder mixed with water, or diluted tinned milk.

Method

  1. Layer lasagna sheets with qeema-tomato sauce, a milk-based white sauce and cheese.
  2. Bake until bubbling and golden.

Not for the air fryer. It is too big for most air fryer baskets. Use the oven.
