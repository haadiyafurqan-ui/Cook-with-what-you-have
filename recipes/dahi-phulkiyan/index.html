Dahi Phulkiyan

40 minIftar & SnacksVegetarian

A short home-style guide to Dahi Phulkiyan. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Gram flour (besan)No gram flour (besan)? Use finely ground chana daal or rice flour (less flavour).
  • Yogurt (Dahi)No yogurt? Use milk with 1 tsp lemon juice, rested 10 minutes.
  • Fresh coriander (Hara dhaniya)No fresh coriander? Use skip it, or use fresh mint.
  • Green chilli (Hari mirch)No green chilli? Use red chilli flakes or extra red chilli powder.

Method

  1. Fry small besan phulkiyan and soak them in warm water, then squeeze and pour over whisked yogurt.
  2. Top with green chutney (coriander, chilli) or tamarind chutney.

Not for the air fryer. The batter is runny, so it drips through the basket. Fry this one.
