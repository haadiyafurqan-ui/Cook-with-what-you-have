Egg Mayo Salad Sandwich

15 minBreakfast & SehriIftar & SnacksQuick (20 min or less)

A short home-style guide to Egg Mayo Salad Sandwich. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Bread (Double roti)No bread? Use roti or paratha as a wrap.
  • Eggs (Anda)No eggs? Use paneer or firm tofu in curries and rice dishes.
  • MayonnaiseNo mayonnaise? Use thick hung yogurt with salt and pepper.

Method

  1. Mash boiled eggs with mayonnaise, mustard, salt, pepper and chopped chives.
  2. Spread between bread slices.
