Chicken Chilli (dry or gravy)

35 minLunch & DinnerAir Fryer

A short home-style guide to Chicken Chilli (dry or gravy), with steps for the usual way and for the air fryer. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Chicken (Murghi)No chicken? Use mutton or beef (cook longer), or soya chunks.
  • CornflourNo cornflour? Use rice flour or plain flour (slightly less crisp).
  • Capsicum (Shimla mirch)No capsicum? Use extra cabbage or onion.
  • Onion (Pyaz)No onion? Use spring onions, or a little onion powder (less depth).
  • Soy sauceNo soy sauce? Use a little vinegar with pepper and extra salt (taste will differ).
  • Green chilli (Hari mirch)No green chilli? Use red chilli flakes or extra red chilli powder.

The usual way: fried

  1. Fry cornflour-coated chicken until crisp.
  2. Stir-fry onion, capsicum, green chilli and garlic with soy and chilli sauce, add the chicken (dry), or a cornflour slurry for gravy.

In the air fryer (about 35 min)

  1. Coat chicken in cornflour, spray with oil and air fry at 200°C for 12 to 14 minutes, shaking halfway, until crisp.
  2. Stir-fry onion, capsicum, green chilli and garlic with soy and chilli sauce, add the chicken (dry), or a cornflour slurry for gravy.

Air fryer tip: Spray the coated pieces with oil before air frying, or the cornflour stays white and powdery.

Air fryer basics: Heat the air fryer for 3 to 5 minutes first. Don't fill the basket more than halfway. Brush or spray a little oil. Turn or shake halfway. Use paper liners with holes. Air fryers vary, so check a few minutes early the first time.
