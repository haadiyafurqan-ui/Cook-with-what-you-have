Seviyan

20 minDessertsSpecial OccasionsQuick (20 min or less)Vegetarian

A short home-style guide to Seviyan. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Vermicelli (Seviyan)No vermicelli? Use broken spaghetti or noodles.
  • Milk (Doodh)No milk? Use milk powder mixed with water, or diluted tinned milk.
  • Sugar (Cheeni)No sugar? Use jaggery (gur) or honey, added gradually.

Method

  1. Lightly fry seviyan in a little oil.
  2. Add milk and sugar and simmer until soft and thickened; serve warm or chilled.
