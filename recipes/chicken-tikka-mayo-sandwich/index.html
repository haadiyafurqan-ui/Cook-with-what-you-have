Chicken Tikka Mayo Sandwich

15 minIftar & SnacksQuick (20 min or less)

A short home-style guide to Chicken Tikka Mayo Sandwich. Basics like salt, oil, haldi, red chilli, cumin, garam masala, ginger-garlic, vinegar, chilli sauce and ketchup are assumed.

Ingredients

  • Bread (Double roti)No bread? Use roti or paratha as a wrap.
  • Chicken (Murghi)No chicken? Use mutton or beef (cook longer), or soya chunks.
  • MayonnaiseNo mayonnaise? Use thick hung yogurt with salt and pepper.
  • Lettuce (Salad patta)No lettuce? Use thinly sliced cabbage.

Method

  1. Shred leftover chicken tikka and mix with mayonnaise.
  2. Fill bread with the mix and lettuce leaves.
